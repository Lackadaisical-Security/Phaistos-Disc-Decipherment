# Phaistos Disc Decipherment

## Overview
The Phaistos Disc is a unique artifact from the Minoan civilization, discovered in 1908 in the Phaistos archaeological site in Crete. Its undeciphered script has intrigued linguists and historians alike for over a century.

## Conclusion
The Phaistos Disc offers a fascinating glimpse into ancient Minoan culture. Continued research will help unravel the mysteries surrounding its inscriptions, contributing greatly to our understanding of ancient scripts.
